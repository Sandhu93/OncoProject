# Consultant confirmation sheet

Purpose: confirm the RT process so the team can build it. Allow 30–45 minutes. Bring [the proposed workflow](rt-workflow-proposal.md) for reference. Record answers with the date and the consultant's name, then copy them into [the decision register](rt-scope.md).

Nothing here asks for clinical content. We only need to know who does what, in what order, and how long each step may wait.

## Part B: Questions we cannot guess

1. **Who assigns the junior resident** for contouring: the consultant, the senior resident, or another person?
2. **Who assigns the physicist,** or does any available physicist pick up the plan?
3. **Who sends the approved plan to the LINAC:** the physicist, the radiographer, or someone else?
4. **If the senior resident finds a problem with the contouring,** does it go back to the junior resident? If the consultant finds a problem, does it go back to the junior resident or to the senior resident?
5. **If the consultant rejects a plan,** does it always go back to the physicist, or sometimes back to contouring?
6. **If contouring changes after a plan is made,** must the plan be redone and re-approved?
7. **Is the "treating doctor" who approves a plan ever different from the consultant?** If yes, who?
8. **Can any step be skipped** (for example, no mould for some patients) or done on the same day (mould and CT)?
9. **Who registers a new patient and RT chart,** and who phones patients who miss an appointment: the consultant, residents, radiographer, or a separate office person?
10. **Does one patient get one RT number per course of treatment?** Does the hospital already have a patient number we must record, and what should the RT number look like?
11. **Where does tracking end:** at the first treatment, at the last treatment, or later? Can treatment be interrupted or stopped, and who records that?

## Part C: Saturday and weekly updates

12. What is a weekly update: a short "all fine / problem" note, a number of sessions completed, or something else? (We will not store clinical findings.)
13. Who must post it: the junior resident, the radiographer, or both every Saturday?
14. By what time on Saturday is it expected? If it is missed, who should be told, and when?
15. What should happen when a Saturday is a holiday?

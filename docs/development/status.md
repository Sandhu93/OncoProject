The chart's answer now says who it is waiting for, which phase it is in, what has been recorded and which steps the signed-in person can take, with each step's fields, defaults and limits, so the web draws the form from the API (`ActionForm`). Done: give mould and CT Sim dates, mould done, CT Sim done (dates cannot be in the past, CT Sim not before the mould, done dates not in the future). Migration `005_chart_actions.sql`. Tests ACT-010, DB-031, WEB-050 (stand-in API) and WEB-051 (real API). Still to do, in order: contouring, planning, treatment steps, then role queues.
- Workflow steps, milestone 7 (contouring): the consultant assigns a junior resident from a pick-list of active, available residents with a due date (recorded as the chart's current `assignments` row; assigning again ends the earlier one), the assigned resident alone submits the contour set (name kept on the chart), a senior resident verifies, the consultant approves. Either may send the chart back with a required reason, which takes back what was submitted, named and verified so it must be done and checked again. Tests ACT-020 and WEB-052.
- Workflow steps, milestone 8 (planning): the physicist submits the plan (plan ID required, kept on the chart), the consultant approves it (as consultant or treating doctor) or sends it back with a reason, which takes back the plan ID, and the physicist sends the approved plan to LINAC 1 or LINAC 2 with a first treatment date and time. The slot is checked against every open chart whose treatment has not started (same machine, date and time), under a database lock so two people reserving at once cannot both get it; the suggested date is the first free weekday. Tests ACT-030 and WEB-053.
- Workflow steps, milestone 9 (treatment): the radiographer tells the patient (refused, saying what is missing, unless contouring is approved, the plan is approved and a LINAC slot is reserved; the same three checks are shown on the form), a junior resident records the start (a treatment record is kept), then, while treatment is in progress, the junior resident and the radiographer each post one Saturday update per week (sessions so far, any gap, a note that is required if there was a gap). A Saturday update is not a stage change: the chart stays put, its version moves on and the history says what was posted; `weekly_updates_due()` no longer lists the chart for that role once posted. A junior resident finishes treatment (a note is required if fewer sessions than planned); the chart is then closed. Every action in the workflow now has a step definition (test ACT-001). Tests ACT-040 and WEB-054.
- Role queues, milestone 10: `GET /charts/queue` returns every open chart marked for the person asking. A chart "needs you" when one of your roles can take a step on it now (for contouring, only the assigned junior resident; in treatment, a role that has not yet posted this week's Saturday update). Each item carries the next step in plain words ("Verify contouring, or send back"), the stage and its position (n of 14), days in the stage (India Standard Time calendar days), a stalled flag (waiting `STALL_DAYS` or more, default 3, never in treatment: D-07 is still to be confirmed), who it is waiting on, the assigned resident, the most useful date ("Contouring due …", "Late: was due …", "Started … · 3 of 25 sessions") and the Saturday updates still missing. The dashboard now leads with "Needs you" (urgent and longest-waiting first, treatment last) and lists everyone else's charts with who they are waiting on; a new Overview page shows four tiles (getting ready, stalled, in treatment, Saturday updates to come), the All / Stalled / Getting ready / In treatment filters with counts, and a progress bar per chart. Tests QUE-010 and WEB-055/WEB-060.
- Notifications, milestone 11: the people a chart is waiting for are told in the app, inside the same transaction as the step, so a person is told if and only if the step was recorded. Told: the radiographers when a chart is issued; whoever owns the next stage on each handoff (the assigned resident for contouring, the role for other stages); the resident, with the reason, when contouring or a plan is sent back; the assigned resident when contouring is given to them (with the due date and who assigned it); the consultants when treatment finishes. The person who did the step is never told about it. A reminder timer (`NOTIFICATION_SWEEP_MINUTES`, default 15) creates, once per person, a "waiting too long" reminder for whoever the chart is waiting for and the consultants, and once a week a "Saturday updates due" reminder for each role that still owes one. The header shows an Inbox link with the unread count (checked every 30 seconds), the Inbox page lists everything with Mark as read and Mark all as read, and the dashboard, overview and inbox refresh themselves every 30 seconds. Email and SMS are not built (decision: in-app only). Tests NOT-010, WEB-056 and WEB-070.
- All 18 transitions of the proposed workflow can be done in the web app by the right role, with the prototype's forms and rules, and each role now has its queue. Not yet built from the prototype: the calendar of appointments, the Saturday-updates page, and the stage map by phase.
- Known gaps: no refresh tokens (sessions last 12 hours), the template's refresh code is switched off. `npm audit` reports transitive findings (`proxy-addr` in the API's Express, `source-map-js` in PostCSS) that predate this change and are not yet resolved.
- Administration (milestone 15): three pages in the main navigation. **Staff** (`/staff`, needs the MANAGE_STAFF access) lists every account with roles, extra access, whether it is switched on or available, and last sign-in; it adds an account (username, full name, roles, extra access and a first password of at least 12 characters), changes a name, roles, access or availability, switches an account off (its sessions end at once) and sets a new password (sessions end and any sign-in lock is cleared). Nobody can switch off or demote their own account, and at least one active account must always be able to manage staff. Every change is audited; the password is never stored in the audit or in the idempotency record. **Workflow** (`/workflow`, anyone signed in) shows the approved workflow to read: who approved it and when, every stage with its phase and Saturday-update roles, and each step out of it with the role that does it. Changing the workflow is still a controlled act, not a screen. **Audit** (`/audit`, needs VIEW_AUDIT) lists the audit trail newest first with who did it, the chart, and the values before and after, filtered by RT number, action, person and dates, in pages of 50. Staff and Audit show in the navigation only to those who hold them, and the pages and the API refuse everyone else. API: `GET/POST /admin/staff`, `POST /admin/staff/:id`, `POST /admin/staff/:id/password`, `GET /admin/workflow`, `GET /admin/audit`. Tests ADM-010 (API), WEB-090 (stand-in API) and WEB-091 (real API). Not built: changing the workflow in the app (CONFIGURE_WORKFLOW), and removing an account (accounts are switched off, never deleted, so history keeps its names).

- Deleting accounts (milestone 15b): `DELETE /admin/staff/:id` removes an account only when nothing has ever been recorded against it (no sign-in, audit entry, chart step, assignment or comment: the database's own references decide, so a table added later is covered). Its roles, access and inbox go with it. Anyone with history is refused (`HAS_HISTORY`) and is switched off instead. Cannot delete yourself or the last account able to manage staff. The audit keeps a `STAFF_DELETED` entry with what the account was. `canDelete` on each account decides whether the screen offers the button. Tests ADM-010, WEB-090, WEB-091.

- Directory sign-in (milestone 15b): off unless `DIRECTORY_URL` is set (see `.env.example`). A person's password is checked by binding to the hospital's LDAP directory as them, using `DIRECTORY_BIND_DN` with `{username}` filled in (for example `uid={username},ou=staff,dc=hospital,dc=local`), so no service account or stored secret is needed. `ldaps://` is expected; `ldap://` is refused unless `DIRECTORY_START_TLS=true`, so a password never crosses the network unencrypted, and the server certificate is always verified (`DIRECTORY_CA_FILE` for a hospital CA). An administrator adds a person as a directory account on the Staff page (no password is set here); the account still carries its roles and access in OncoTrack. Only the stored login name is ever put into the DN, an empty password is never sent (it would be an anonymous bind), a wrong password counts toward the same lock as any other, and an unreachable directory answers 503 and is not counted against the person. A password cannot be set here for a directory account. Not built: looking people up in the directory, or taking roles from its groups; both need the hospital's directory layout. Tests DIR-001, DIR-002 (unit), DIR-010 (API), WEB-090. Not tested against a real directory server yet: the first pilot must try one account against the hospital's directory.

## Repository organization

The original numbered planning files now live under `docs/archive/initial-planning/`. The assessment is in `docs/planning/`, and the startup guide is in `docs/development/`. Use [the documentation index](../README.md) to find current guidance. The reorganization preserves application paths and standard root-level tooling configuration.

- Final updates after treatment (milestone 11b, from the doctor's step 13): completing treatment no longer closes the chart. It moves to `TREATMENT_COMPLETED`, where the junior resident and the radiographer each post one final update (`POST_FINAL_UPDATE`, stored with kind `FINAL`, one per role, not editable). The second one moves the chart to the new terminal stage `TRACKING_ENDED` and closes it; the first leaves it where it is. Both roles are told (and the queue shows the chart to whoever has not posted); the consultants are told when tracking ends. Migration `007_final_updates.sql` upgrades an already-activated proposed workflow; run `npm run db:migrate` on existing dev databases (also for 006). Tests DB-030, ACT-040, NOT-010, QUE-010, WEB-054.
- Comments and handoff notes (milestone 12): every chart page has a Discussion section. Anyone signed in can add a comment (up to 1,000 characters, operational only, also on a closed chart), optionally for one role. That role is told; with no role, whoever the chart is waiting for is told (the assigned resident in contouring). Every step that passes a chart on also offers an optional "Note for the next person" (up to 500 characters, not offered on send-backs, which carry their reason, or on Saturday and final updates). It is kept in the discussion, marked as a handoff note, and sent with the "your turn" notice. Migration `008_chart_comments.sql` (run `npm run db:migrate` on existing dev databases); `POST /charts/by-number/:rt/comments`; tests COM-010, WEB-031 and WEB-051.
- Consultant controls (milestone 13): only a consultant sees "Consultant controls" on a chart, each with a required reason, recorded in the history and audit trail, and with the people it affects told. They are sent to the same `POST /charts/by-number/:rt/actions/:action` endpoint. `REASSIGN_CONTOURING` (while in contouring: the new resident is told, and the one it was taken from, who can no longer submit), `RETURN_TO_STAGE` (to an earlier stage, only before treatment starts; whoever it goes back to is told why), `DISCONTINUE_CHART` (status Discontinued, no more steps; the people it was waiting for and the other consultants are told) and `REOPEN_CHART` (a discontinued chart goes back to its stage). Nobody else can use them (403). No migration. Tests CTL-010 and WEB-056.
- Calendar, Saturday updates and stage map (milestone 14): three new pages in the main navigation. **Calendar** (`/calendar`, `GET /charts/calendar?from&days`) shows mould, CT Sim and first-treatment appointments by day for a fortnight (stepping forward and back), with machine for first treatment, a tick for what is recorded as done, and a "Not recorded as done" list for past dates nobody recorded. It reads the dates held on open charts, so it cannot disagree with a chart. **Saturday updates** (`/saturday`) shows, for the week ending, who has posted on every chart in treatment, which are yours to post, and which finished charts still owe final updates. **Stages** (`/stages`, from `stages` in `GET /charts/queue`) lists every stage of the active workflow in order by phase with the charts in each and how many are stalled. Not built: a mould or CT Sim slot clash check (only the LINAC first slot is checked) and room or machine capacity. Tests CAL-010, WEB-080.
